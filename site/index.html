One shortcut. Nothing to configure.

Hold to talk.
Tap to keep talking.

Recording starts the instant you press, so your first word is never cut off while Dimmela works out what you meant.

Hold

For a quick thought.

Keep fn space down while you speak. Let go and it's pasted.

Lock

For when you have a lot to say.

Tap once and talk hands-free, for as long as you like. Tap again to finish.

Esc

Changed your mind?

Press esc and nothing is pasted, nothing is kept. An accidental tap with no speech costs nothing either.

Modes

Your prompts and skills,
a digit away.

Point Dimmela at the folders where you keep prompt files and skills (SKILL.md). Turn on the ones that make sense for dictation and drag them into order: the order is the digit. Edit a file and the next Dictation uses it.

Transcript

Post-processing goes to any OpenAI-compatible provider, such as OpenRouter or a model running on your own machine. Every Dictation starts with your Default Mode, which can be none. If the provider is unreachable, you get the Transcript and a notification, so you never lose what you said.

On-device

Your voice never leaves your Mac.

Speech is transcribed by Parakeet v3 on the Neural Engine. No account, no server, and it works offline.

  • Audio stays local.Recordings are transcribed and kept only for your last five Dictations.
  • Post-processing is opt-in.Only text is sent, and only to the provider you choose.
  • Your key, in your Keychain.Not in a preferences file.

No more “ehm”

Fillers like “uhm” and “mmh” disappear. Words with meaning, like “cioè” or “tipo”, never do.

History

Your last five Dictations, with their audio. Copy one, or Post-process it again with another Mode.
